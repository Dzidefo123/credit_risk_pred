## Field definitions and boundaries

- **orig_credit_score**: Provider origination credit score; do not equate to VantageScore or time-current score.

- **first_payment_month**: First scheduled payment month; not exact origination, recognition or observation entry.

- **first_time_buyer**: Official pinned R47 first time buyer

- **maturity_month**: Disclosed scheduled maturity month; not observed payoff date.

- **msa**: Official pinned R47 msa

- **mi_percentage**: Official pinned R47 mi percentage

- **number_units**: Official pinned R47 number units

- **occupancy_status**: Official pinned R47 occupancy status

- **orig_cltv**: Official pinned R47 orig cltv

- **orig_dti**: Official pinned R47 orig dti

- **orig_upb**: Official pinned R47 orig upb

- **orig_ltv**: Official pinned R47 orig ltv

- **orig_interest_rate**: Official pinned R47 orig interest rate

- **channel**: Official pinned R47 channel

- **prepayment_penalty**: Official pinned R47 prepayment penalty

- **amortization_type**: Official pinned R47 amortization type

- **property_state**: Official pinned R47 property state

- **property_type**: Official pinned R47 property type

- **postal_prefix**: Coarsened source postal geography; private only, no public facility geography.

- **loan_id**: Source mortgage facility identifier; combine with vintage. Never a borrower identifier.

- **loan_purpose**: Official pinned R47 loan purpose

- **original_loan_term**: Official pinned R47 original loan term

- **number_of_borrowers**: Official pinned R47 number of borrowers

- **seller_name**: Official pinned R47 seller name

- **super_conforming**: Official pinned R47 super conforming

- **pre_harp_loan_id**: Source linkage field; not general borrower linkage.

- **special_program**: Official pinned R47 special program

- **harp_indicator**: Official pinned R47 harp indicator

- **valuation_method**: Official pinned R47 valuation method

- **interest_only**: Official pinned R47 interest only

- **vantage_score**: Separate disclosed VantageScore field; absence is not a substitute score.

- **loan_id**: Source mortgage facility identifier; combine with vintage. Never a borrower identifier.

- **reporting_month**: Official pinned R47 reporting month

- **current_principal_balance**: Official pinned R47 current principal balance

- **delinquency_state**: Monthly categorical state; XX unknown, RA REO, numeric bands. Not exact daily DPD.

- **loan_age**: Provider mortgage age in monthly intervals; audited against first-payment proxy, not overwritten.

- **remaining_legal_months**: Official pinned R47 remaining legal months

- **defect_month**: Official pinned R47 defect month

- **modification_flag**: Disclosed modification indicator; absence does not prove no historical intervention.

- **termination_code**: Provider zero-balance code; 01 combines payoff/maturity, 02/03/09 research-default causes, 15/16/96 administrative.

- **termination_month**: Reported zero-balance month; missing without termination is NOT_APPLICABLE. Mismatch with reporting month is ambiguous.

- **current_interest_rate**: Official pinned R47 current interest rate

- **non_interest_upb**: Official pinned R47 non interest upb

- **last_paid_due_month**: Official pinned R47 last paid due month

- **mi_recoveries**: Official pinned R47 mi recoveries

- **net_sale_proceeds**: Aggregate sale proceeds; alpha disclosure codes preserved in raw cache, not guessed as zero.

- **non_mi_recoveries**: Official pinned R47 non mi recoveries

- **total_expenses**: Official pinned R47 total expenses

- **legal_costs**: Official pinned R47 legal costs

- **preservation_costs**: Official pinned R47 preservation costs

- **taxes_insurance**: Official pinned R47 taxes insurance

- **misc_expenses**: Official pinned R47 misc expenses

- **actual_loss**: Signed provider actual-loss disclosure; aggregate audit only, not discounted workout cash flow.

- **cumulative_modification_costs**: Official pinned R47 cumulative modification costs

- **rate_step_indicator**: Official pinned R47 rate step indicator

- **payment_deferral_flag**: Disclosed deferral flag; reporting scope may depend on servicing regime.

- **estimated_ltv**: Official pinned R47 estimated ltv

- **removal_upb**: Official pinned R47 removal upb

- **delinquent_accrued_interest**: Official pinned R47 delinquent accrued interest

- **disaster_flag**: Official pinned R47 disaster flag

- **assistance_plan**: Disclosed assistance plan; no inference of complete forbearance coverage.

- **period_modification_costs**: Official pinned R47 period modification costs

- **interest_bearing_upb**: Official pinned R47 interest bearing upb

- **mi_cancelled**: Official pinned R47 mi cancelled

- **servicer_name**: Official pinned R47 servicer name

- **bankruptcy_cramdown_costs**: Official pinned R47 bankruptcy cramdown costs

- **vintage**: Prespecified archive vintage; not inferred borrower cohort.

- **research_key**: Composite (vintage, source_facility_id); private identity only.

- **event_category**: Unchanged Task2 default/payoff/administrative/ambiguous/unknown categories.

- **analytical_prefix**: First contiguous known active history; stops at gap, duplicate, unknown or endpoint; no re-entry.

- **post_research_endpoint**: Quarantine flag after first observed research endpoint; raw observations retained.

- **post_source_termination**: Quarantine flag after first source termination; raw observations retained.

- **pandemic_regime**: Calendar flag for 2020/2021; not actual forbearance or causal treatment.

- **months_since_first_payment_proxy**: Reporting month minus scheduled first-payment month; not exact mortgage age.

- **borrower_id**: No usable borrower linkage supplied; do not assume independence.

- **exact_origination_date**: Not supplied by first-payment month.

- **timed_recovery_cashflows**: Aggregate loss disclosures cannot supply timed recovery cash flows.

- **exact_days_past_due**: Monthly delinquency bands cannot be harmonized to exact daily DPD.



No historical semantic change is declared absent merely because current-release columns match. Entirely blank fields are MISSING_IN_SOURCE; this is not evidence of STRUCTURALLY_UNAVAILABLE.
