# External replication feasibility — Task 12

Fannie Mae is a plausible future replication source: its primary dataset provides origination attributes and monthly performance for a selected conventional fixed-rate mortgage population. It excludes several products and separately provides HARP data. Access requires registration and acceptance of the provider's terms. Released attributes can be corrected and updated after modifications. These differences require an explicit harmonization contract, rather than assuming Freddie and Fannie loans or events are interchangeable. [Official product information, checked 2026-10-07](https://capitalmarkets.fanniemae.com/credit-risk-transfer/fannie-mae-single-family-loan-performance-data).

The recommended next task is a feasibility and harmonization contract covering:

- Original/current coupon units, modification timing and historical knowledge-time limitations.
- Acquisition versus origination/first-payment entry, seasoning and eligible risk sets.
- Default, payoff/maturity, repurchase, administrative exit and censoring semantics.
- PIT mortgage-rate coverage and supported follow-up horizons.
- Population disjointness and dependence through shared macro calendar regimes.
- Deterministic selection and a new sealed validation ledger before fitting or inspecting outcomes.

No external records were acquired in Task 12. Acquisition and external replication remain separate future authorizations. Task 12's decision remains exploratory even if its results favor the refinancing hypothesis.
